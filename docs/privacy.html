Privacy policy

Budget for Android · updated 25 November 2026

What the app keeps, and where

Budget keeps your operations, categories, merchants, plans and settings in a database on your phone only. There is no account, no server of ours and no advertising. Nothing is sold or shared.

Bank SMS and notifications

With your permission the app reads SMS and app notifications from your bank (TBC Bank) to record card operations. They are read on the phone; messages from other senders are not read or kept. Bank messages the app could not understand are kept on the phone for 30 days so that you can mark them.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Only if you turn on «На Google Диске» (automatic copy to Google Drive), the app asks you to sign in with Google and to allow access to its own hidden app folder on your Drive (scope drive.appdata). It cannot see or change any other file on your Drive. The copy is encrypted on the phone with your password (AES-256-GCM, key derived with PBKDF2) before it is uploaded; neither Google nor we can read it. Your email address is used only to show which account the copies go to. You can turn it off at any time; the app's data on Drive can be deleted in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Crash reports

Release builds send crash and error reports to Firebase Crashlytics (Google): the error's text and stack trace, the app version, the phone model and Android version. Amounts, merchants, bank messages and other contents of your budget are not included.

Updates and configuration

The app asks GitHub for the latest release to offer updates, receives a notification about a new version through Firebase Cloud Messaging (a topic subscription, no personal data), and downloads message templates for your bank through Firebase Remote Config. Currency rates are downloaded from the National Bank of Georgia.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it keeps on the phone. Copies you saved to a folder or to Google Drive stay there until you delete them.

Кратко по-русски

  • Все данные бюджета хранятся только на телефоне; аккаунта и своего сервера нет, рекламы и продажи данных нет.
  • SMS и уведомления читаются только от банка, на телефоне.
  • Вход через Google — только для копии на Google Диске: доступ лишь к скрытой папке приложения; копия шифруется вашим паролем до отправки.
  • Отчёты о сбоях (Crashlytics) — текст и стек ошибки, версия, модель телефона; без сумм, мерчантов и SMS.
  • Удалить приложение — удалить все его данные на телефоне.
